# Philippines terms and privacy policy draft

Draft for product and Philippine legal review. This is not the currently accepted policy and must not be published as final until the release decisions at the end are resolved and the implementation and acceptance migration are verified. References to features describe repository capabilities; confirm which are enabled at launch.

## Terms and Conditions

### Our service and these terms

Let's Be Friends is a platform for adults to discover Companions, communicate, arrange platonic experiences online or in person, and use the related community and payment features available in the service. These terms apply to use in the Philippines. Your rights under applicable Philippine law are not waived by these terms. Our Privacy Policy explains how we process personal data.

### Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 years old and provide accurate information. Use only an account you are authorized to use, keep your sign-in credentials secure, and do not impersonate someone else or evade a restriction. Notify us if you believe your account has been compromised. Some actions require identity verification or an approved Companion profile. Verification helps us review identity and eligibility; it is not a guarantee of a person's conduct or safety.

### Respect, boundaries and safety

The service is for platonic companionship and shared activities. Sexual services, sexual exploitation, coercion, harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, fraud and illegal activity are prohibited. Respect each person's boundaries and their decision to decline or end an interaction. Do not request or share intimate images without consent, record another person without appropriate permission, or disclose someone else's private information without a lawful basis. Do not use the service to facilitate trafficking or exploitation.

Choose meeting arrangements carefully and make your own assessment of the activity and location. We cannot guarantee the conduct of users or safety of an online or in-person session. The service is not an emergency response service. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency services.

### Profiles, discovery and community content

Profiles, availability and discovery results depend on information provided by users and may be incomplete or outdated. Distance and location information are approximate. Do not scrape the service, create fake profiles, manipulate discovery or reviews, spam users, distribute malicious content, or interfere with the service.

You remain responsible for content you post, upload or send, including photos, comments, messages, polls and reviews. Only share content you have the right to use. You allow us to host, use and display your content as needed to operate and protect the service, including displaying it to the audience for the feature you use. This permission lasts as needed for those purposes and is subject to applicable law and our retention practices. Other users may retain information they have already seen or received. Reviews must reflect a genuine booking experience and must not be misleading, abusive or retaliatory.

Circles, events and community features are subject to these same rules. An event listing or membership does not establish that an event, venue or organizer has been endorsed or independently checked by us.

### Bookings and experiences

A booking request is not confirmed until the Companion accepts it and the service shows confirmation. Review the activity, session type, time, location, price and any displayed fees before requesting. Members and Companions should communicate relevant expectations and boundaries. Do not pressure someone to agree to an activity outside the agreed experience. Each person is responsible for their own lawful conduct and expenses unless the booking expressly states otherwise.

The app may allow cancellation before specified stages of a booking. After a completion confirmation has been recorded, the report or dispute process may be required instead. Check the booking screen for available actions and the applicable outcome before confirming. If a booking is disputed, we may review relevant information and pause unsettled funds while the matter is assessed. Applicable consumer rights remain available.

### Payments, wallet and withdrawals

Prices, fees and total charges shown before confirmation form part of the transaction. Funding a wallet, paying for a booking, receiving earnings and withdrawing available funds may depend on identity checks, account status, provider availability and applicable limits. Funds may be reserved for a booking or withdrawal and therefore unavailable for another transaction while it is pending. Settlement and withdrawal status are shown in the service. A failed transfer may be returned to the available wallet balance according to the displayed transaction status. Provider processing times and availability can vary.

Use only payment methods and payout accounts you are authorized to use. We may investigate suspected fraud, incorrect payments or unauthorized activity and restrict transactions or pause unsettled funds where reasonably necessary and permitted by law. Report a transaction problem promptly. The payment provider may apply its own terms. The service will show applicable charges before the relevant confirmation.

### Companions

Companions control their availability, lawful rates, activities and boundaries, and should accept only bookings they can safely fulfill. They are responsible for their own applicable taxes, licenses and legal obligations. The legal relationship between a Companion and the platform is determined by applicable law and the actual working arrangement, not by a label alone.

### Referrals

A referral reward is subject to the eligibility, qualifying activity, amount and any limits displayed in the service. Do not create accounts to refer yourself, mislead invitees, spam or otherwise manipulate rewards. We may investigate and reverse rewards issued because of fraud or error, subject to applicable law.

### Reports and enforcement

Use the available report tools to report concerning profiles, messages, bookings or reviews. Provide accurate information and do not submit knowingly false reports. We may review relevant content and evidence, remove content, restrict features, suspend an account, preserve records or pause unsettled funds when reasonably necessary for safety, investigation or legal reasons. Actions and disclosures are subject to applicable law. A report does not guarantee a particular outcome. Do not use reporting as a substitute for emergency assistance.

### Changes and applicable law

We may update these terms as the service or legal requirements change. Any notice, effective date and acceptance process for material changes will be communicated as appropriate and required by law. Philippine law applies, subject to mandatory rights and remedies available under applicable law. Nothing in these terms excludes liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Privacy Policy

### Scope

This policy describes personal data processed when you use Let's Be Friends in the Philippines. It should be read together with the Terms and Conditions. Personal data may be collected directly from you, generated through your use of the service, or received from service providers and other users, such as when someone submits a report.

### Information we process

- Account and profile information, including sign-in details, username, display name, bio, broad area, profile content and Companion information.
- Approximate location and discovery information. Coordinates saved through onboarding are rounded to two decimal places. Approved Companions may appear in nearby discovery using rounded coordinates.
- Identity review information, including government ID images, a current selfie and confirmed legal name, date of birth, ID type, and where provided or shown, nationality and ID expiry date.
- Booking and transaction information, including participants, session details, communications, prices, wallet entries, payment status, payout details, withdrawal history and dispute evidence.
- Content and activity, including messages and attachments, posts, comments, reviews, media, reactions, follows, saved items, poll votes, Circle membership and event participation.
- Referrals and rewards, notifications, device and push-delivery identifiers, reports, safety preferences, administrative records and security or audit logs as relevant to the features used.
- Information you or others provide in a report, support request or investigation. This may include sensitive personal information. Please avoid sharing unnecessary information about yourself or another person.

### Why and on what basis we use data

We use personal data to provide accounts and features, show relevant discovery results, arrange bookings, communicate, process payments and payouts, verify eligibility, prevent fraud, maintain security, investigate reports, enforce rules and meet legal obligations. Depending on the processing, our basis may include your consent, steps necessary to perform an agreement, compliance with a legal obligation, or a legitimate interest where permitted by Philippine law and subject to your rights. Sensitive personal information requires an applicable basis under the Data Privacy Act. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable law and the effect on a feature that requires the data.

### Visibility and sharing with other users

Your username, display name, bio and broad area may be public. Approved Companion profiles may appear on nearby discovery with rounded location. Exact meeting details stay private until the Companion accepts a booking. Messages and booking information are available to the relevant participants; content posted to community features is visible according to the feature and audience. Recipients may keep copies of information you share. ID images, ID details and selfies do not appear on public profiles and are not visible to the other booking participant.

### Providers and identity review

We use providers for functions such as authentication, hosting, maps, payment processing, payouts, notifications and security. We disclose data to them as needed for their services and subject to applicable safeguards. Provider arrangements may involve processing outside the Philippines.

For identity review, the ID image is sent to an OpenAI field reader to extract printed fields and assess legibility. Storage is disabled for that request, although provider safety logs may apply. The selfie remains in private storage and is not sent to that field reader or used as biometric proof. A human safety team makes the decision on each completed identity submission. Authorized staff may access relevant records for verification, safety and investigation. We may also disclose information where necessary and lawful for safety, legal claims, legal requirements or a business transfer.

### Retention and security

ID images and selfies are retained while your account exists for identity checks, safety, fraud prevention and incident reports. After account removal they are deleted on scheduled review unless an active incident review or documented legal hold requires continued retention; earlier deletion may be required by law. Access to ID images is limited to authorized reviewers and administrators for verification or an active report concerning the member, and access is time limited and audit logged.

Other records are retained as needed for their purposes, including delivering the service, resolving disputes, security and legal recordkeeping, subject to applicable law. Some records may remain after a feature stops being used or an account is removed where a lawful reason requires or permits retention. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable Philippine law, you may have rights to be informed, access and correct your data, object to processing, request erasure or blocking, obtain data portability, seek damages and complain to the National Privacy Commission. You can correct certain profile details in the service. Withdrawing consent or objecting to processing may affect features that depend on the data; it does not necessarily affect processing based on another lawful ground. We may need to verify a request and retain information where law permits or requires it. You may raise a privacy concern with the National Privacy Commission through its published complaint process.

### Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in processing or applicable law. We will communicate material changes and seek consent where required. The version and effective date will be shown with the policy.
