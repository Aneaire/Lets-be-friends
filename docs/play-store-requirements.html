02 / Timeline

The critical path is the testing gate

A personal account adds a mandatory closed test. An organization account skips it, at the cost of entity verification.

Account setup
1 to 3 days
Preview QA sign-off
1 day
Production AAB
1 day
Closed test, 12 testers
14 continuous days
Production review
1 to 7 days

Personal account: roughly 3 to 4 weeks end to end.

Organization account: roughly 1 to 2 weeks, plus D-U-N-S lead time.

06 / Policy

Declarations that block review

Must answer

  • Data safety form
  • Content rating questionnaire
  • Target audience and age
  • Ads declaration
  • Financial features declaration

Must provide

  • Account deletion page and in-app path
  • Reviewer test login, Google OAuth only today
  • Permission justifications, location and photos
  • User content reporting and blocking, already built
  • Payments statement, PayMongo for real world services

Google sign-in only means review needs a working test account. Plan one Google account for reviewers before submitting.

09 / Expo

What Expo handles, what it cannot

Handles

  • Cloud builds and signing
  • Keystore and credential storage
  • Submission to a Play track
  • Per-environment variables
  • Over-the-air updates, not installed yet

Cannot

  • Create or verify the Play account
  • Waive the 12 tester gate
  • Fill policy declarations
  • Manage the Play listing
  • Guarantee review approval

Free plan covers this launch: 15 Android builds and store submission included.
