Internal reference01
The cost

PayMongo charges us in two places only.

Money coming in costs a percentage. Money going out costs a flat fee. Everything else is free.

Money in / QR Ph top-up
1.50%

1.34% plus 12% VAT on the fee. On PHP 1,000 added, PayMongo keeps PHP 15.00.

Money out / InstaPay withdrawal
PHP 10 flat

Whatever the amount. Withdrawing PHP 100 or PHP 5,000 costs the same PHP 10.

Payouts are free. The 1.50% never changes with the size of the payment, so large top-ups are not penalized.
The cost02
The price

We charge the member in two places.

Adding money is free for the member. We pay the 1.50% ourselves.

On every booking
15%

Added on top of the Companion's rate. A PHP 1,000 session costs the member PHP 1,150, and the Companion still earns the full PHP 1,000.

On every withdrawal
PHP 10

Added on top of the amount, never taken out of it. Withdrawing PHP 500 costs PHP 510 from the balance, and PHP 500 lands in the bank.

Top-ups stay free for the member. The withdrawal fee is the only new charge, and it is the only one the member controls.

Earnings05
The fix

A top-up then withdrawal used to cost us PHP 10.75.

Before
− PHP 10.75
We paid PayMongo's PHP 10 withdrawal fee and absorbed the PHP 0.75 QR Ph fee on the top-up.
After
− PHP 0.75
The member pays the PHP 10. We still absorb the PHP 0.75 because BSP puts the QR Ph merchant fee on us.
The round trip is no longer a loss. A member can add money and withdraw it again, and the only cost left is the QR Ph fee we would pay on any booking.
