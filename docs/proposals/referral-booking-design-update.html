Let's Be Friends The rule

One promise the whole product can explain.

A member shares a link. A friend joins and books. The referrer earns 5% of the booking subtotal, for life.

The friend pays the normal price. The credit comes out of the 15% service fee, so the platform keeps the remaining 10 points of that fee.

Referral and booking design update 02 / 08
Let's Be Friends Referrer · Web

The dashboard answers three questions.

What do I share, who joined, and what have I earned. Everything else is secondary.

  • Share. One link and one code, with copy in the same block.
  • Track. Friends joined, rewarded bookings, and referral credit available.
  • Understand. Every reward states its status in words: pending release, under review, available, reversed.
  • Trust. The 30-day claim window and the subtotal-only basis sit next to the link.

Let's Be Friends Mobile · Parity

The same rules, in the hand.

The native app follows the web wording, status model, and money math.

  • Referral credit available reads the wallet sub-balance. Lifetime credited stays visible beside it.
  • Requests for you sit first, then active Companion bookings, then history.
  • Detail pages repeat subtotal, service fee, and total, and name the platform fee.
  • Shared links open the app and land on the claim card. The add action stays explicit.
Referral and booking design update 06 / 08
Let's Be Friends Design rules

Six rules behind the screens.

The detail work follows from these, not from decoration.

01One price, one promise

No discount theater. The friend pays the normal price and the page says so.

02Show the math

Subtotal, fee, total, and the referral share appear in the same place.

03Status in words

Pending release, under review, reversed. Color never carries meaning alone.

04Accent with meaning

Blue for self and account actions. Pink for social and booking actions.

05Keep referral private

No public badge or marker. The referral record stays server-side.

06Safety is visible

Held credit says why it is held and who can resolve it.

Before

5% of the service fee, launch bonus banner, three-booking cap.

After

5% of the booking subtotal, lifetime, no bonus, one action.
