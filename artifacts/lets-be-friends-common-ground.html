Let's Be Friends product design research

Common Ground

A compact, trust-first social application that borrows Facebook's predictable app grammar and Nextdoor's local usefulness, then replaces engagement clutter with clearer planning, safety, and real-world connection.

The design decision

Do not reskin the current UI. Rebuild the application shell and its core surfaces around one job: help a member move confidently from discovery to a shared plan.

01
Use a familiar shell, not familiar incentives.

Facebook proves the value of stable navigation, a central feed, a lightweight composer, and a dedicated messaging workspace. Nextdoor proves the value of local context, verified participation, useful recommendations, and safety-aware community prompts. Let's Be Friends should adopt those interaction principles without copying their brand, visual language, ad patterns, or attention loops.

Research synthesis

What each product teaches us

The useful overlap is not a generic social feed. It is a dependable application frame, clear relevance context, and a short path from seeing something useful to acting on it.

NextdoorLocal utility

Trust is strongest when context is visible.

Nextdoor's 2025 redesign reorganized the product around Alerts, News, and Ask. The important pattern is prioritization by local utility, not the specific modules.

  • Show why something is relevant nearby.
  • Place verified identity and source context near the content.
  • Use recommendations to answer a real need.
  • Add respectful friction before harmful community actions.

Evidence: Nextdoor's July 2025 redesign, Kindness and moderation features.

FacebookApp grammar

Consistency lets a large product feel learnable.

Facebook separates algorithmic Home from connection-led Feeds, keeps core destinations in a shortcut bar, and treats messaging as its own focused workspace.

  • Keep primary destinations stable on every screen.
  • Separate discovery from content a member deliberately follows.
  • Expose feed reasons and preference controls in context.
  • Make the common creation path obvious and advanced tools secondary.

Evidence: Home and Feeds, 2025 Feed and navigation update.

Deliberate rejectionProduct fit

Do not import attention economics.

Let's Be Friends succeeds when members make safe, suitable plans, not when they maximize time in a feed.

  • No Stories or Reels rail.
  • No public popularity race or oversized reaction counts.
  • No generic local news, marketplace, or alert subsystem.
  • No exact location exposure in social discovery.
  • No growing menu of weakly related destinations.

Each destination has a distinct job and interaction model. This keeps the product compact without forcing unrelated features into the feed.

Core journey

Design around the plan, not the post

The main product journey should remain legible across feed, discovery, profile, booking, and messaging. The Plan Line supplies continuity.

01Set intent

Choose an activity, mode, time, and comfort preference.

02Explore people

Compare Friend Hosts using fit, Strengths, boundaries, and availability.

03Review trust

See identity, review, safety, price, and location privacy context together.

04Suggest a plan

Send a complete booking request with a plain-language summary.

05Coordinate

Continue in Messages where changes and booking state stay visible.

Shape

14px cards, 9px controls, circular avatars, one-pixel neutral rules. Reserve pills for filters, states, and compact metadata. Avoid ornamental containers, glass effects, and gradients.

Accent logic

Blue marks identity, account, setup, and personal configuration. Pink marks booking, messaging, following, reviewing, and other member-to-member actions. Neutral is the default. Danger remains red and explicit.

Density and spacing

Compact means less repetition

The design becomes denser by removing ceremony and duplicate labels. Important controls remain comfortably operable and content keeps a readable rhythm.

Layer
Target rhythm
Rule
Application chrome

48 to 56px bars

Persistent but visually quiet. Only primary destinations and account status remain visible.

Interactive target

40 to 44px preferred

Never trade compactness for hard-to-hit controls. WCAG 2.2 sets a 24 by 24 CSS pixel minimum or sufficient spacing.

Card padding

12 to 16px mobile, 16 to 20px desktop

Use spacing to separate information groups inside the card, not to make every card feel oversized.

Stack gap

10 to 12px feed, 16 to 24px sections

Frequent items stay close. A larger gap signals a new task or context.

Body copy

15 to 16px, 1.5 to 1.6 line height

Keep feed text readable and cap long excerpts. Expand only on deliberate entry to detail.

Responsive behavior

One-dimensional at 320px

Cards, filters, and flows must reflow without a horizontal reading dependency.

Accessibility references: WCAG 2.2 target size, WCAG 2.2 reflow, and consistent navigation.

Interaction details

Small choices that change the feel

These details make the application feel purpose-built rather than like a neutral social template.

Feed cards explain themselves

Every recommended item gets one short reason, such as “Because you saved Board games” or “Available near your approximate area.” That reason opens directly into preference controls in the implemented product.

Plans are a content type

A member can suggest a plan from Home without creating a generic post first. Plan cards contain activity, mode, time range, approximate place, and desired company.

Trust stays next to the decision

Identity, Friend Host approval, boundaries, price, and privacy are summarized beside the booking action. Safety information does not live only on a separate page.

Reactions remain human-scale

Use “Appreciate” instead of a competitive reaction set. Hide aggregate counts until there is meaningful activity. Emphasize replies and next steps over popularity.

Notifications have jobs

Group activity into Needs action, Conversations, and Community. Only Needs action may create a persistent dot. Let members choose a digest for lower-priority activity.

Kindness adds one useful pause

If a message or comment appears harmful, show a neutral reflection prompt before sending. Keep report and block controls reachable without making every conversation feel alarmed.

Risks and decisions

What to protect during implementation

A clean-sheet visual refactor can still preserve the product's working trust model. These are the main failure modes to watch.

High
A feed becomes the product instead of the doorway.

Measure success by profile consideration, suitable booking requests, completed plans, and safe repeat interactions. Do not optimize for raw session time.

High
Local context exposes too much location information.

Use city, approximate distance, and member-controlled discovery visibility. Do not adopt Nextdoor's address model or a precise social map.

Medium
Compact density hides trust information.

Compress repeated explanation, not verification, boundaries, price, cancellation, reporting, or location privacy.

Medium
Facebook familiarity makes the product feel copied.

The Plan Line, intent-led composer, person-first Explore, neutral palette, and restrained reactions must remain visibly specific to Let's Be Friends.

Medium
A visual rewrite accidentally changes contracts.

First implementation should reuse existing routes, queries, mutations, and booking states. Any new plan-post schema or notification persistence needs separate approval.
