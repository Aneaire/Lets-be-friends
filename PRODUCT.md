# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Let's Be Friends serves adults who want safe friendly companionship, online conversations, local activities, and shared experiences. Primary users are members looking for Friend Hosts, Friend Hosts who offer approved activities, and admin reviewers who keep verification, moderation, and booking safety moving.

## Product Purpose

The product is a trust-first friend hosting and booking app. It helps members discover approved Friend Hosts by strengths, safe categories, availability, and location while keeping identity checks, admin approval, location privacy, and reporting visible in the flow.

Success means people can understand who they are interacting with, what kind of interaction they are starting, and which trust gate applies before a booking or social action proceeds.

## Brand Personality

Simple, trustworthy, friendly, direct.

The interface should feel like a calm product surface for real decisions, not a playful dating app or a cold labor marketplace.

## Anti-references

- Dating-app styling or swipe-first interaction patterns.
- Marketplace language that makes people feel like products.
- Decorative gradients, loud color blocks, glass effects, and ornamental motion.
- Playful UI that weakens trust around booking, identity, reporting, or admin review.
- Generic emerald, teal, beige, or cream SaaS palettes.

## Design Principles

1. Safety is visible.
2. People are not products.
3. Actions reveal intent through color.
4. Use simple UI over decorative UI.
5. Keep trust, booking, and profile flows readable before making them expressive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web app should target WCAG AA contrast. Light and dark mode must both be readable, keyboard-accessible, and usable without relying on color alone. Blue and pink accents indicate action intent, but labels, icons, and position must still make the action clear.
